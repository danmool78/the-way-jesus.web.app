I Am the Bread of Life

A deeper food for those who came looking for a full stomach

Capernaum · AD 29, spring · Jn 6:22–71

Story

The next day, the crowd that had eaten the bread crossed by boat to Capernaum looking for Jesus. He saw straight into their hearts. You are looking for me not because you saw signs, but because you ate the bread and were filled. Do not work for food that spoils and is gone, but for food that lasts forever.

The people brought up the manna their ancestors ate in the wilderness. Jesus answered that he himself was the true bread that comes down from heaven and gives life to the world. A murmur spread through the synagogue at Capernaum. Isn't this the son of Joseph? How can he tell us to eat his flesh?

Saying this teaching was too hard, many of his disciples left and no longer walked with him. Jesus turned to the Twelve who remained. Do you want to leave too? After a moment of silence, Peter spoke. You have the words of eternal life, Lord. To whom would we go?

Background

The white limestone synagogue that stands in Capernaum today is thought to date from the fourth century or later, but the black basalt foundation beneath it may be a trace of the synagogue of Jesus' day.
