Teaching on the Mountain

A voice on the hillside that overturned the world's wisdom

Mount of Beatitudes · AD 28, summer · Mt 5:1–7:29 · Lk 6:17–49

Story

A hillside overlooking the Sea of Galilee. Seeing the crowds, Jesus went up the mountain and sat down, and his disciples drew close. On a slope where the wind swept through the grass, a teaching began that turned the world's wisdom upside down. Blessed are the poor in spirit.

You are the salt and the light of the world. Love your enemies and pray for those who persecute you. Pray to your Father who sees in secret. Look at the birds of the air and the lilies of the field. Do not worry about tomorrow, but seek first the kingdom of God and his righteousness.

The teaching ended with a story of two houses. Whoever hears these words and does them is like a house built on rock, which does not fall when the rain pours and the wind beats against it. The crowds were amazed, for he taught them as one with authority, not like their scribes.

The sayings are fresh renderings of the original meaning in today's language. Text: Mt 5:1–7:29 · Lk 6:17–49

Background

Matthew presents this as a teaching given on a mountain, Luke as one given on a level place. The Church of the Beatitudes on a hill north of the Sea of Galilee was built in 1938, following a tradition that honored this spot as the site of the sermon.
