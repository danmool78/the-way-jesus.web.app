Also known as · Psalm 148 · Hallelujah

Jerusalem · Psalm 148

Story

The third of the five Hallelujah songs calls on the whole world. It summons in turn the angels of heaven, sun, moon, and stars, the seas, mountains, trees, and beasts of the earth, kings and peoples, the old and the young, and tells them all to praise the name of the Lord together.
