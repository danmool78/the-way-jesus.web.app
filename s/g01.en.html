God's Love

God loves you

Garden of Eden · In the beginning · Gen 1:26–2:8

Story

First, God loves you, and he has a good purpose in mind for your life. The Bible puts it this way. God loved the world so much that he gave his only Son, so that everyone who trusts in him will not be lost but will have life that never ends.

In the beginning God made light, and the sky, and the earth. He shaped a human being from the dust and breathed the breath of life into him. You are not here by accident. You were made to walk with God and to live inside his love.

Jesus said, I came so that people may have life, and have it overflowing. So why do so many people never come to know this love and this full life?

Background

Genesis says that people were made in the image of God (Gen 1:27). The whole Bible runs as one story: God loves people and calls them into a life lived together with him.
