Psalm 79 · The Nations Have Entered Your Inheritance

The day Jerusalem fell

Also known as · Psalm 79 · O God, the nations

Jerusalem · Psalm 79

Story

It was the day Jerusalem became a heap of rubble, and there was no one to bury those who had fallen in the streets. This song bearing Asaph's name is the prayer of those left in the ruined city, crying out to God, How long?

The song (in English)

O God, the nations
have come into your inheritance
They have defiled your holy temple
and turned Jerusalem into rubble

They gave your servants' bodies to the birds
the flesh of your faithful to wild beasts
They poured out their blood like water
and there was no one to bury them

How long, O Lord?
Will you be angry forever?
Do not hold our fathers' sins against us
Let your mercy come quickly to meet us
for we have been brought very low

O God of our salvation
help us for the glory of your name
Let the prisoners' groans come before you
Preserve those condemned to die
Remember the blood your servants shed

How long, O Lord?
Let your mercy come quickly to meet us

We are your people, your pasture's sheep
We will thank you forever
We will tell your praise to every generation

Hear the song and walk the place →

Refrain

How long, O Lord? Will you be angry forever?
