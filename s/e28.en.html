Rebuking the Wind and the Sea

Who is this man?

Out on the Sea of Galilee · AD 28, autumn · Evening · Mt 8:23–27 · Mk 4:35–41 · Lk 8:22–25

Story

As the day full of parables drew to a close, Jesus said, Let us cross to the other side. Leaving the crowd behind, the disciples took him along in the boat, and other boats set out with them. Then a furious squall arose, and the waves broke over into the boat.

The water was rising, yet Jesus was asleep in the stern with his head on a cushion. The disciples woke him. Teacher, do you not care that we are about to die? Even the voices of these fishermen, raised on the sea, were shaking with fear.

Jesus got up, rebuked the wind, and said to the sea, Quiet. Be still. The wind ceased, and the sea lay calm as a mirror. Why are you so afraid? Do you still have no faith? Filled with an even greater fear, the disciples asked one another, Who then is this?

Background

The Sea of Galilee lies in a basin about two hundred ten meters below sea level. When cool air from the surrounding highlands suddenly rushes down the valleys, a calm lake can be whipped into a violent storm in moments.

Pause and Reflect

When Jesus seems to be asleep in the storms of my life, which do I hold on to, fear or faith?
