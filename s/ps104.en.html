Psalm 104 · The World He Made

Sea and mountains, beasts and people — a song of creation

Also known as · Psalm 104 · Bless the Lord, O my soul

Night Sky over the Wilderness · Psalm 104

Story

No author's name remains on this song. The singer recalled the beginning, when God, wearing light like a garment, laid the foundations of the earth and set a boundary for the sea. Then he praised him, calling out one by one the wild donkeys of the hills, the birds in their nests, and even the ships on the sea.

The song (in English)

Bless the Lord, O my soul
Wrapped in light as with a robe
stretching out the heavens like a tent
he walks on the wings of the wind

He set the earth on firm foundations
so it will never be shaken
He set a boundary for the waters
and barred them from crossing the line

He makes springs flow in every valley
and the wild donkeys quench their thirst
The birds sing among the branches
He gave wine to gladden the heart

O Lord, the works of your hands
how wonderful they are
In wisdom you created them all
The earth is full of your works

All of them look to you alone
and receive their food in due season
When you hide your face, they tremble
When you send your Spirit, they live anew
and the whole earth comes alive again

O Lord, the works of your hands
how wonderful they are
I will sing to the Lord all my life
I will praise God every moment

Bless the Lord, O my soul
Hallelujah
