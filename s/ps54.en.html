Psalm 54 · Betrayed in the Wilderness of Ziph

The Ziphites tell Saul where David is hiding (1 Sam 23:19)

Also known as · Psalm 54 · Save me, O God, by your name

Wilderness of Ziph · Psalm 54

Story

While David was hiding in the thickets of the wilderness of Ziph, the Ziphites went up to Gibeah and told Saul where he was hiding. His own fellow men of Judah handed him over. It is the song of the day Saul's army came closing in around the hill.
