The Alley at Dusk

Above all else, guard your heart

The Alley at Night · In the days of King Solomon · Night · Prov 7:1–27; 4:23

Story

My son, it's late, so let's go home by this alley. Once, looking out through the lattice of our window, I saw among the simple, among the young men, a young man lacking sense. He was passing along the street near the corner of her house, in the twilight, in the evening, in the black dark of night.

Then a woman with a crafty heart came out to meet him. She coaxed him with smooth words and whispered that her husband had gone on a long journey and was not at home. At once the young man followed her, like an ox going to the slaughter, like a bird darting into a snare, not knowing it will cost him his life.

My son, do not let your heart turn aside to her ways; do not stray into her paths. Above all else, guard your heart, for from it flow the springs of life. Say to wisdom, You are my sister, and call understanding your close kin. Then wisdom will guard you and keep you from the woman who leads you astray with her words.

Background

Proverbs 7 is a short story in which a father tells what he saw through the lattice of his window. Houses in ancient Israel had wooden lattices instead of glass, so those inside could see out while those outside could hardly see in. In the very next chapters (8–9), Proverbs portrays another woman calling in the streets: Wisdom.
