Psalm 102 · Prayer of the Afflicted

In days like ashes

Also known as · Psalm 102 · O Lord, hear my prayer

Rivers of Babylon (far to the east) · Psalm 102

Story

The heading calls this song the prayer of one who is afflicted, who grows faint and pours out his complaint before the Lord. Since it cherishes even the stones and dust of Zion as it waits for the time when God will rebuild it, it was probably a lament sung in the days of exile.

The song (in English)

O Lord, hear my prayer
Let my cry come to you
Do not hide your face in my day of distress
Answer me quickly when I call

My days vanish like smoke
My bones burn like glowing coals
My heart withers like grass
I even forget to eat
I am like an owl in the wilderness
like a lonely sparrow on the roof
I eat ashes like bread, I drink tears

But you, O Lord, are enthroned forever
Your name endures to all generations
You will arise and have mercy on Zion
for now the time to show favor has come

Your servants cherish her stones
and pity even her dust
He will regard the prayer of the destitute
and hear the groaning of the prisoner

Heaven and earth wear out like a garment
but you remain the same
and your years will have no end

Your servants' children will live in peace
their offspring will stand firm before you

You remain the same

Hear the song and walk the place →
