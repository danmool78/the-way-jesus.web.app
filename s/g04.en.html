The Response of Faith

Welcome him in by faith

Mount of Olives · And today · Acts 1:6–11

Story

Fourth, each one of us needs to welcome Jesus Christ as our Savior and as the Lord of our life. Then we come to know God's love and live in it. The Bible says, to all who welcomed him, to those who believed in his name, he gave the right to become children of God.

Welcoming him is not something we earn by doing good. It is God's gift, received by faith. It means turning away from running my own life, and placing my life in Jesus' hands. Jesus said, look, I am standing at the door and knocking. If anyone hears my voice and opens the door, I will come in to them. Right now, who is in charge of your life?

If this prayer is what your heart wants to say, pray it with me now. Jesus, I am a sinner. I have turned away from God and lived my own way. I believe that you died on the cross for me and rose to life again. Right now I open the door of my heart. Please come in, forgive my sins, and be the Lord of my life. Thank you for taking me in as a child of God. I pray in your name, Jesus. Amen.
