The Centurion's Faith

Only say the word

Capernaum · AD 28 · Mt 8:5–13 · Lk 7:1–10

Story

In Capernaum there was a centurion whose servant, dear to him, was sick and dying. Hearing about Jesus, the centurion sent some Jewish elders to ask for help. The elders pleaded earnestly. He loves our nation, they said, and he even built our synagogue for us.

As Jesus drew near the house, friends sent by the centurion delivered his words. Lord, do not trouble yourself. I am not worthy to have you come under my roof. Only say the word. I too am a man who takes orders and gives them.

Jesus was amazed, and he turned to the crowd following him. Not even in Israel, he said, had he found such faith. When the messengers returned to the house, they found the servant already well. A single word had crossed the distance and reached a life.

The sayings are fresh renderings of the original meaning in today's language. Text: Mt 8:5–13 · Lk 7:1–10

Background

A centurion commanded about a hundred soldiers. Galilee was then the territory of Herod Antipas, so this man was most likely a Gentile officer serving under Antipas.

Pause and Reflect

Without any visible proof, can I trust him completely and ask him only to say the word?
