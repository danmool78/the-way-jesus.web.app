The Procession of Palms

A king on a young donkey, weeping over the city

Mount of Olives · Holy Week · Sunday · Mt 21:1–11 · Mk 11:1–11 · Lk 19:28–44 · Jn 12:12–19

Story

At the foot of the Mount of Olives, on the road near Bethphage and Bethany. Jesus sends two disciples into the village ahead to bring back a young donkey that no one has ever ridden. When they lay their cloaks on its back, he quietly mounts the small animal.

Pilgrims who have come for Passover spread their cloaks on the road and wave palm branches. "Hosanna! Blessed is the one who comes in the name of the Lord!" The shout rolls down the slopes of the Mount of Olives to the city walls. The Pharisees press him to rebuke his disciples.

But when the road turns downhill and the city spreads out before him, Jesus weeps in the middle of the procession. This cheering city had not recognized the way to peace, and was walking toward the ruin that was coming.

Background

Zechariah 9:9 foretold that the king of Zion would come humbly, riding on a donkey. Matthew and John read this entry as the fulfillment of that prophecy. Palm branches stood for national victory and liberation.

Pause and Reflect

Do I welcome Jesus as the king I want him to be, or as the king he came to be?
