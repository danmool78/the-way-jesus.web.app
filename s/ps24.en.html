Psalm 24 · The King of Glory

The ark comes up to the City of David (2 Sam 6)

Also known as · Psalm 24 · The earth and all that fills it

Jerusalem · Psalm 24

Story

The ark of the Lord, which had stayed three months in the house of Obed-edom, at last came up to the City of David. David, wearing a linen ephod, danced with all his might, and all Israel shouted for joy and blew the trumpets. This song of questions and answers at the city gates was likely sung in such a procession.
