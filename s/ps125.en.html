Psalm 125 · Jerusalem Ringed by Mountains

A pilgrim song for going up to Jerusalem

Also known as · Psalm 125 · Those who trust in the Lord

Mount of Olives · Psalm 125

Story

Looking up from inside the city, they could see the mountains surrounding Jerusalem. The Mount of Olives to the east and the hills to the south and west held the city in their embrace. Looking at those mountains, the pilgrims sang that the Lord surrounds his people.

The song (in English)

Those who trust in the Lord
are like Mount Zion
They cannot be shaken
They stand forever

As the mountains surround Jerusalem
the Lord surrounds his people
from this time on and forever
he surrounds his people

The scepter of the wicked
will not remain on the land of the righteous
so that the righteous may not reach out
their hands to do wrong

O Lord, do good to those who are good
to those who are upright in heart

As the mountains surround Jerusalem
the Lord surrounds his people

Peace be upon Israel
