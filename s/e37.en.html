Transfigured on the Mountain

Glory glimpsed for a moment on the mountain

Mount Tabor (traditional) · AD 29, summer · Mt 17:1–13 · Mk 9:2–13 · Lk 9:28–36

Story

Six days later, Jesus took only Peter, James, and John and went up a high mountain. As he prayed, his face shone like the sun and his clothes became dazzling white. When the drowsy disciples opened their eyes, Moses and Elijah were talking with Jesus.

They were speaking of his departure, which he was about to accomplish in Jerusalem. Flustered, Peter blurted out: Lord, it is good for us to be here. Let me build three shelters. At that moment a bright cloud covered them, and a voice rang out from the cloud. This is my beloved Son. Listen to him.

The disciples fell face down and trembled. Someone laid a hand on their shoulders. Get up, do not be afraid. When they looked up, they saw only Jesus. Coming down the mountain, Jesus told them to tell no one what they had seen until the Son of Man had risen from the dead.

Background

The Gospels say only 'a high mountain.' Tradition has long identified it as Mount Tabor, but many scholars point to Mount Hermon, which is near Caesarea Philippi from the previous scene and much higher.

Pause and Reflect
