The Well at Sychar

Noon at the well, two thirsty people

Sychar · Jacob's Well · Late AD 27 · Jn 4:1–42

Story

Leaving Judea for Galilee, Jesus passed through Samaria and came to a town called Sychar, where Jacob's well was. Tired from the road, he sat down by the well. It was around noon, with the sun directly overhead. His disciples had gone into town to buy food.

When a Samaritan woman came to draw water, he asked her for a drink. The conversation began with her astonishment that a Jew would ask a Samaritan woman for water. It went on to living water that ends thirst forever, to her five husbands, and to the question of where God should be worshiped.

When the woman said the Messiah was coming, he told her, I am he, the one speaking to you. She left her water jar behind and ran into town to call the people. Many Samaritans came and urged him to stay, and he remained two days. This man, they declared, is truly the Savior of the world.

Background

Samaritans worshiped on Mount Gerizim rather than in Jerusalem, and there was deep hostility between them and the Jews. Jacob's well, which still survives near modern Nablus, is said to be about 40 meters deep.
