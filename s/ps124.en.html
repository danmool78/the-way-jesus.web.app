Psalm 124 · If the Lord Had Not Been on Our Side

A pilgrim song for going up to Jerusalem

Also known as · Psalm 124 · If the Lord had not been on our side

Jerusalem · Psalm 124

Story

The pilgrims sat together in a circle and talked of days gone by. There had been days when enemies swept over them like a flood and they were nearly swallowed up. When one person began this song, handed down as a psalm of David, everyone joined in.

The song (in English)

If the Lord had not been on our side
let Israel now say
when people rose up against us
If the Lord had not been on our side

when their anger flared like fire
they would have swallowed us alive
The waters would have engulfed us
the torrent would have swept over our souls

Praise be to the Lord
who did not give us to their teeth
Like a bird escaping the fowler's snare
the snare is broken and we are free

Praise be to the Lord
the snare is broken and we are free
Our help is in the name of the Lord
in the name of the Maker of heaven and earth

the Maker of heaven and earth
