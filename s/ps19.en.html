Psalm 19 · The Heavens Speak

The sky and the Word — two books

Also known as · Psalm 19 · The heavens tell the glory of God

Night Sky over the Wilderness · Psalm 19

Story

The sun rose at the eastern edge and ran from one end of the sky to the other. David sang that the silent heavens declare God's handiwork day after day, and then he sang of the Lord's law that revives the soul. It is a song of two books: the sky and the Word.

The song (in English)

The heavens tell the glory of God
The sky proclaims the work of his hands
Day passes the news on to day
Night hands its wisdom on to night

There is no sound and there are no words
yet their echo spreads through all the earth

The sun, like a bridegroom from his chamber
rejoices like a warrior running his course
From one end of the heavens to the other
none can hide from its light

The law of the Lord is perfect
reviving my soul again
The testimony of the Lord is sure
making the simple wise

Words more precious than the finest gold
Words sweeter than honey from the comb
Wash away my hidden faults
Keep me from stubborn sins

The law of the Lord is perfect
He revives my soul again
O Lord, my rock and my redeemer
May the words of my mouth and my heart
be pleasing in your sight

My rock and my redeemer
