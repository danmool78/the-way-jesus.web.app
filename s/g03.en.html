Jesus, the Only Way

Jesus Christ alone is the way

Golgotha · Garden Tomb · Around AD 30 · Jn 19:16–20:18

Story

Third, Jesus Christ is the one way God has made to deal with our sin. Only through him can we know God's love and live in it. Jesus said, I am the way, the truth, and the life. No one comes to the Father except through me.

Jesus, the Son of God, became a human being and came to us. Then on the cross at Golgotha, the one who had no sin paid the price of our sin in our place. The Bible says, while we were still sinners, Christ died for us.

At dawn on the third day, the tomb was empty. Jesus had beaten death and risen to life again, and he appeared to many people. Now he has become the one and only bridge between God and us.
