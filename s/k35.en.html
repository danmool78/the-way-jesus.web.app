Night in the Assyrian Camp

What happened in a single night

Jerusalem · c. 701 BC · 2 Kgs 19:35–37

Story

That night Jerusalem held its breath. The great Assyrian army was still camped in the land of Judah, and the only thing the people in the city had to hold on to was the word Isaiah had brought them. The Lord had said, “The king of Assyria will not enter this city; he will go back the way he came.” Holding on to that one word, the people stayed awake through the long night.

That very night the angel of the Lord went out and struck the Assyrian camp. When people got up early in the morning, a hundred and eighty-five thousand soldiers never rose again. The army that had seemed ready to swallow Judah collapsed in a single night. Only a heavy silence settled over the camp.

Sennacherib king of Assyria withdrew his army and went back to Nineveh. Later, while Sennacherib was worshiping in the temple of his god Nisroch, two of his sons killed him, and another son, Esarhaddon, became king. One of the psalms sings it this way: be still, and know that I am God.

Background
