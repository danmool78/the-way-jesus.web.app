Psalm 29 · The Voice over the Waters

The voice of the Lord from Lebanon to the wilderness of Kadesh

Also known as · Psalm 29 · Give to the Lord, O sons of God

Wilderness of Kadesh · Psalm 29

Story

A storm rolled in from the sea, broke the cedars of Lebanon, and swept south as far as the wilderness of Kadesh. In that thunder David heard the voice of the Lord. Calling on that voice seven times, he sang for the heavenly beings to give glory to God.
