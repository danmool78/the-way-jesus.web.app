Psalm 68 · God Arises

God marching from Sinai to Zion

Also known as · Psalm 68 · God arises

Mount Sinai · Psalm 68

Story

Every time the ark set out, Moses cried, Arise, O Lord! This song of David begins with that cry and sings of God marching from Mount Sinai through the wilderness to the sanctuary in Zion. Singers and young women playing tambourines followed the procession.

The song (in English)

God arises
and his enemies scatter
As smoke is blown away by the wind
those who hate him flee before him

For him who rides through the desert
build a highway and sing
His name is the Lord
Rejoice and dance before him

Father of the orphan, judge for the widow
God in his holy dwelling
He gives the lonely a home
and leads the prisoners out

When you went out before your people
marching through the wilderness
the earth shook, the heavens poured rain
even Sinai trembled before you

The Lord who bears our burdens daily
Praise the God of our salvation
The singers go in front
the musicians follow behind
Between young women with tambourines
my King enters the sanctuary

Sing to God, O kingdoms of the earth
Praise the Lord who rides the clouds
His name is the Lord
Rejoice and dance before him

Praise God
