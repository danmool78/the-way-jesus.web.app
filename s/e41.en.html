The Good Samaritan and Two Sisters

One who becomes a neighbor, one who sits at his feet

Bethany · AD 29, autumn · Lk 10:25–42

Story

An expert in the law stood up to test Jesus. What must I do to inherit eternal life? When the man himself recited the law to love God and neighbor, Jesus told him to do this and he would live. He asked again: And who is my neighbor?

Jesus told of the rugged road that goes down from Jerusalem to Jericho. A man was attacked by robbers and left half dead, and a priest and a Levite both passed by on the other side. But a Samaritan traveling that way saw him, was moved with compassion, and went to him. He poured oil and wine on his wounds, bandaged them, brought him to an inn, and cared for him.

As Jesus traveled on, he stopped at Martha's house in a certain village. Martha was busy with all the serving, while her sister Mary sat at the Lord's feet listening to his words. When Martha poured out her frustration, Jesus gently called her name twice. Only one thing is truly needed.

Background

From Jerusalem to Jericho was about twenty-seven kilometers, a wilderness descent of more than a thousand meters. Luke says only 'a certain village,' but John's Gospel identifies the village of Martha and Mary as Bethany.
