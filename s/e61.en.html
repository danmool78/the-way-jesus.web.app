Into the Cloud

A blessing and a promise left at his parting

Mount of Olives · Forty days after the resurrection · Lk 24:50–53 · Acts 1:6–11

Story

For forty days after the resurrection Jesus has been appearing to the disciples and speaking about the kingdom of God. Now he leads them out as far as the outskirts of Bethany. The disciples ask one last question. Lord, is this the time you will restore the kingdom to Israel?

He answers that the times and seasons rest with the Father, and then tells them that when the Holy Spirit comes on them, they will be his witnesses to the ends of the earth. Then, lifting his hands to bless them, he is taken up into heaven. A cloud hides him, and they see him no more.

As the disciples stand gazing at the sky, two men in white stand beside them and speak. This Jesus, who has been taken into heaven, will come back in just the way you saw him go. Filled with great joy, the disciples return to Jerusalem and praise God every day in the Temple.

The sayings are fresh renderings of the original meaning in today's language. Text: Lk 24:50–53 · Acts 1:6–11

Background

Acts says that after the Ascension the disciples returned from the Mount of Olives to Jerusalem, a Sabbath day's walk away. Bethany is a village on the eastern slope of the Mount of Olives.
