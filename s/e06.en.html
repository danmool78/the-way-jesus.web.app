Return to Nazareth

A child growing up in an unknown village

Nazareth · After 4 BC · Mt 2:19–23 · Lk 2:39–40

Story

When Herod died, the angel came again to Joseph in a dream in Egypt. Those who sought the child's life were dead, so he was to return to the land of Israel. Joseph took the child and his mother and started for home.

But word came that Archelaus, Herod's son, was ruling Judea in his father's place. Afraid, Joseph was warned again in a dream, turned north toward Galilee, and settled in a town called Nazareth.

In that small village on the hill, the child grew. Luke notes briefly that he grew strong and was filled with wisdom, and that the grace of God rested on him. Quiet years passed, far from the eyes of the world.

The sayings are fresh renderings of the original meaning in today's language. Text: Mt 2:19–23 · Lk 2:39–40

Background

Herod the Great is thought to have died around 4 BC, after which Archelaus ruled Judea and Herod Antipas ruled Galilee. Archelaus ruled so harshly that Rome removed him in AD 6.
