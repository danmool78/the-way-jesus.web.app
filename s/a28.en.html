You Will Not See My Face Again

The elders who wept and walked him to the ship

Miletus · c. AD 57 · Acts 20:13–38

Story

Paul was hurrying to reach Jerusalem before Pentecost. So he did not stop at Ephesus but sailed past it and landed at Miletus. Paul sent to Ephesus and called for the elders of the church. When the elders came, Paul said, “You know how I served the Lord with humility and tears from the first day I came to Asia.”

Paul went on, “Now, bound by the Spirit, I am going to Jerusalem. In every city the Holy Spirit warns me that chains and troubles are waiting for me. But my life means nothing to me, if only I can finish the work of telling the gospel of God's grace. Now none of you will ever see my face again.”

Paul urged them, “Savage wolves will come in and attack the flock, so stay alert. The Lord Jesus himself said, ‘It is more blessed to give than to receive.’” When he had finished speaking, Paul knelt down and prayed with them all. The elders wept loudly, threw their arms around Paul's neck, and kissed him. Then they walked him to the ship.

Background

Miletus was a port city a little more than fifty kilometers south of Ephesus. Here Paul looked back on his three years in Ephesus, when he taught the believers night and day with tears.
