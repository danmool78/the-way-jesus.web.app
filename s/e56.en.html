Mary

The voice that calls her by name

Golgotha · Garden Tomb · First day of the week · Dawn · Mt 28:1–10 · Mk 16:1–8 · Lk 24:1–12 · Jn 20:1–18

Story

The first day of the week, at dawn, while it is still dark. Mary Magdalene comes to the tomb and finds the stone moved from the entrance. At her news, Peter and the beloved disciple come running, out of breath. Inside the empty tomb lie only the linen cloths, and the face cloth folded up by itself.

After the disciples go home, Mary stays outside the tomb, weeping. Bending to look in, she sees two angels in white sitting where the body had lain, one at the head and one at the feet. She turns, and someone is standing there, but through her tears she takes him for the gardener.

"Mary." A familiar voice speaks her name. Turning, she cries out, Rabboni, which means Teacher. She runs to the disciples and tells them she has seen the Lord. On the morning of the resurrection, that joy spread outward from the lips of one woman.

Background

Magdala was a fishing town on the western shore of the Sea of Galilee. Luke says Jesus drove seven demons out of Mary Magdalene, and that she supported Jesus and his companions out of her own means.
