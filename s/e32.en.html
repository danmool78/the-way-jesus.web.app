The One Who Comes Over the Water

On a windy night, he comes walking on the water

Out on the Sea of Galilee · AD 29, spring · the fourth watch of the night · Mt 14:22–33 · Mk 6:45–52 · Jn 6:16–21

Story

After sending the crowds away, Jesus had the disciples get into the boat and go ahead of him to the other side, and he went up the mountain alone to pray. As the night deepened, a headwind rose. For hours the disciples rowed in the dark, but the boat was still being battered by the waves in the middle of the lake.

In the fourth watch, while the sky was still black, someone was walking toward them across the water. The disciples screamed that it was a ghost, and then a familiar voice reached them. "Take heart. It is I." Peter shouted back: Lord, if it is you, tell me to come to you on the water. "Come."

Peter stepped out of the boat and walked a few steps on the water. But when he saw the fierce wind, fear swept over him and he began to sink. Lord, save me. At once Jesus reached out his hand and caught him. When the two of them climbed into the boat, the wind died down, and the disciples fell down before him.

Background

In the Roman division of the night, the fourth watch ran from about three to six in the morning. John writes that the disciples saw Jesus after they had rowed twenty-five or thirty stadia, roughly five or six kilometers.
