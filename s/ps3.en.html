Psalm 3 · Barefoot up the Mount of Olives

Fleeing from Absalom (2 Sam 15:30)

Also known as · Psalm 3 · O Lord, how many are my foes

Mount of Olives · Psalm 3

Story

News reached Jerusalem that his son Absalom had been made king in Hebron. David covered his head and climbed the road up the Mount of Olives barefoot and weeping. This is the song the hunted king sang that night.

The song (in English)

O Lord, how many are my foes
So many rise up against me
Many are saying of my soul
God will not save him

But you, O Lord, are a shield around me
my glory, the one who lifts my head
When I cry aloud to the Lord
he answers me from his holy hill

I lay down and slept, and woke again
for the Lord sustained me
Though tens of thousands surround me
I will not be afraid

Arise, O Lord
Save me, O my God

But you, O Lord, are a shield around me
my glory, the one who lifts my head
Salvation belongs to the Lord
Let your blessing be on your people

Let your blessing be on your people

Hear the song and walk the place →
