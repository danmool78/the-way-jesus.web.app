The Day the Roof Opened

The faith of friends opened the roof

Capernaum · AD 28 · Mt 9:1–8 · Mk 2:1–12 · Lk 5:17–26

Story

Word spread that Jesus was back in Capernaum, and the house where he was staying filled in an instant. There was no room even at the door. Pharisees and teachers of the law from every corner of Galilee and Judea, and from Jerusalem, were sitting there among them.

Then four men came carrying a paralyzed man on a mat. Finding the way in blocked, they climbed onto the roof, scraped away the earth, and broke through. Through the falling sunlight and dust, the man was lowered on his mat right down in front of Jesus.

Seeing their faith, Jesus declared that the man's sins were forgiven. The scribes muttered to themselves that this was blasphemy. Then Jesus told him to get up and walk, and in front of everyone he picked up his mat and walked out. All of them praised God, saying they had never seen anything like this.

The sayings are fresh renderings of the original meaning in today's language. Text: Mt 9:1–8 · Mk 2:1–12 · Lk 5:17–26

Background

Houses in Galilee usually had flat roofs of branches and clay laid over wooden beams, reached by an outside stair. Luke describes the man being let down through the tiles.

Pause and Reflect

For someone near me who cannot come on their own, am I willing to break through a roof if that is what it takes to bring them to Jesus?
