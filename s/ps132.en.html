Psalm 132 · A Vow for the Ark

Until David found a place for the ark

Also known as · Psalm 132 · O Lord, remember David

Jerusalem · Psalm 132

Story

For a long time the ark stayed in the house of Abinadab in the woods of Kiriath-jearim. David swore to the Lord that he would not lie down in comfort until he had found a place for the ark. In later years, pilgrims going up to the temple remembered David's vow with this song.

The song (in English)

O Lord, remember David
and all the hardships he endured
He swore an oath to the Lord
He made a vow to the Mighty One of Jacob

I will not enter my house
nor go up to my bed
until I find a place for the Lord
I will not let my eyes sleep

We heard of it in Ephrathah
We found it in the fields of the woods
Let us go into his dwelling place
Let us bow down before his footstool

Arise, O Lord, go to your resting place
you and the ark of your might
Let your priests be clothed in righteousness
Let your saints shout for joy

For the Lord has chosen Zion
He has made it his dwelling
This is my resting place forever
Here I will dwell, he said

Arise, O Lord, go to your resting place
you and the ark of your might
There I will make a horn grow for David
and set a lamp for my anointed

This is my resting place forever

Hear the song and walk the place →
