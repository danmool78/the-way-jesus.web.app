I Am the Good Shepherd

The shepherd who calls his sheep by name

The Temple · AD 29, winter · Feast of Dedication · Jn 10:1–42

Story

The shepherd comes in through the gate and calls his own sheep one by one by name, and the sheep know his voice and follow him out. Jesus borrowed this familiar scene to speak of himself. I am the gate for the sheep. I am the good shepherd. The good shepherd lays down his life for the sheep.

A few months later, in winter, it was the Feast of Dedication. As Jesus was walking in Solomon's Porch in the Temple, people gathered around him. How long will you keep us in suspense? If you are the Christ, tell us plainly. Jesus answered: My sheep hear my voice, and no one can snatch them out of my hand.

The Father and I are one. At these words they picked up stones again. Jesus slipped out of their grasp and withdrew across the Jordan, to the place where John had first baptized, and stayed there. And there many believed in him.

Background

The Feast of Dedication is a winter festival commemorating how Judas Maccabeus, in 164 BC, recovered the Temple defiled by the foreign king Antiochus the Fourth and dedicated it again. Today it is Hanukkah.
