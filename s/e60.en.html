The Last Charge on the Mountain

The promise to be with them to the end of the age

A Mountain in Galilee · After the resurrection · Mt 28:16–20

Story

The eleven disciples climb the mountain in Galilee where Jesus told them to go. In the wind that brushes the ridge, each carrying his own failures and fears, they wait for him at the appointed place. It was just as the message given to the women at the tomb had said.

When at last he appears, the disciples fall down and worship. Yet Matthew honestly records that some of them still doubted. Jesus comes to them, just as they are, and speaks.

All authority in heaven and on earth belongs to him. So go and make disciples of all nations, baptize them, and teach them. To a small band of mingled doubt and faith, the whole world is entrusted. And one last promise remains. I am with you always.

Background

Matthew's Gospel opens with the Immanuel prophecy, God is with us, and closes with the promise, I am with you always. Which mountain this was is not recorded.

Pause and Reflect

Even with doubts still in me, can I walk the road he has entrusted to me?
