Psalm 41 · The Friend Who Shared My Bread

The upper room: Judas's betrayal (John 13:18)

Also known as · Psalm 41 · Blessed is the one who cares for the poor

The Upper Room · Psalm 41

Story

At the last Passover supper, after washing the disciples' feet, Jesus said that this was to fulfill the Scripture, The one who eats my bread has lifted his heel against me. Judas took the piece of bread and went out at once into the night.

The song (in English)

Blessed is the one who cares for the poor
The Lord will rescue him in days of trouble
Even on his sickbed
the Lord will sustain him

O Lord, be gracious to me
for I have sinned against you
Heal my soul

Even my close friend
whom I trusted, who shared my bread
has turned against me
and lifted his heel against me

My enemies whisper about me
When will he die and his name perish?
They come to see me and speak lies
then go out and spread them abroad

But you, O Lord, be gracious to me
and raise me up again

In my integrity
you uphold me
and set me in your presence
forever

Praise the Lord, the God of Israel
from everlasting to everlasting, Amen
